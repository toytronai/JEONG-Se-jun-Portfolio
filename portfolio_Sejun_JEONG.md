## About Me

I am a data-driven problem solver with a deep passion for databases, healthcare, and social impact.

Throughout my career, I have contributed to business growth and real-world social problem solving through data, technology, public policy, and entrepreneurship.

My personal experience with lymphoma and hyperthyroidism led me to recognize the fundamental importance of health in human life. This experience inspired me to pursue AI Healthcare and Gerontechnology, focusing on how data and artificial intelligence can improve health outcomes, care delivery, and quality of life.

Today, I work at the intersection of healthcare, data science, public policy, and human behavior, with a particular interest in aging populations, chronic disease management, digital health innovation, and social welfare data.

I am not simply interested in analyzing data. I am passionate about transforming data into meaningful solutions that create measurable impact.

> "Leading the future of healthcare through AI and data."
